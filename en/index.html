How to find Carrer Fènix, 13

Red prawns today

Aristeus antennatus · the Dénia red prawn

It lives on muddy seabeds hundreds of metres deep, in the sea between Dénia and Ibiza. Here it is served boiled, the way this house has always served it.

Pepe Piera, “El Pegolí”, was one of the first restaurateurs to champion it. He was honoured as the pioneer who made the Dénia red prawn known, and in 2014 he received a recognition at the International Creative Cooking Competition of the Dénia Red Prawn.

How the price works

Sold by weight

Red prawns are priced by weight, like in the fish market. The price changes with the catch and the season, and goes up at Christmas.

Ask first

Ask for today’s price and order in grams. That way you know what you will pay before it reaches the table.

Other seafood

The fish of the day and shellfish also depend on what comes into the fish market.

The table

In the old order

For years there was a single menu here, always in the same order: salad and salted fish first, then the seafood and, to finish, the arròs a banda. Today you can order à la carte or follow that order.

The house

On the same rock since 1943

A tribute to the bar’s old matchboxes, which carried the names of its founders.

  1. 1943

    A small beach bar opens in Les Rotes: the Baret de les Rotes, run by J. Piera Sendra and A. Puchol Llopis. A hand-painted sign above its slatted counter read: “Hay cerveza y aperitivos” (beer and snacks).

  2. Pepe

    Their son, José Piera Puchol, came from the town of Pego, hence the nickname “El Pegolí”. He turned the bar into the house of red prawns and arròs a banda, and the nickname became the restaurant’s name.

  3. 2014

    He is recognised at the International Creative Cooking Competition of the Dénia Red Prawn.

  4. 2016

    He donates to the town of Dénia the archaeological pieces the sea had given him over the years: amphorae that came up tangled in fishing nets, and a Napoleonic-era Carrara marble bust he found while diving for octopus.

  5. 2017

    Pego names him an adopted son of the town.

  6. 2019

    Pepe “El Pegolí” dies in August, aged 76.

  7. Today

    The third and fourth generations of the Piera Sendra family carry on, on the same rock in Les Rotes. El Pegolí is a recommended restaurant in the Guía Repsol 2026.

The setting

Les Rotes, Dénia’s rocky coast

Les Rotes is the stretch of coast running from Dénia harbour towards Cap de Sant Antoni: sandstone rock, clear water and small coves. It is a protected plant micro-reserve and sits next to the Cap de Sant Antoni marine reserve. The restaurant is about 2 km from Dénia town centre.

Before you come

Good to know

  • Lunch only

    Tuesday to Sunday, 13:00 to 16:00. We don’t open in the evening. Closed on Mondays.

  • Book by phone

    +34 965 78 10 35. There is no online booking. In summer and at weekends, call well ahead.

  • Priced by weight

    Red prawns, shellfish and the fish of the day follow the fish-market price. Ask the price before ordering.

  • Parking

    Parking in Les Rotes is hard in summer. There is free street parking, but allow extra time.

  • Accessible

    The restaurant is adapted for people with reduced mobility.

  • With children

    High chairs and baby changing facilities.

  • With your dog

    Pets are allowed. Let us know when you book.

  • Gluten-free and vegan

    There are options for coeliacs and vegans. Tell us when you call.

  • Groups

    Group lunches and celebrations: give us a call and we’ll talk it through.

  • Holidays

    We close for a few weeks in winter. If you are coming in December or January, call first.
